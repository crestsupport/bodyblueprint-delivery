Movement: Signal, Don’t Just Burn

In the Beginner Tier, you moved to build consistency. Now, you’ll move to send signals — to your mitochondria, your hormones, and your nervous system. This isn’t about burning calories. It’s about activating GLP-1 and building metabolic resilience.

🫀 Zone 2 Cardio

Train at a pace where you can still speak in full sentences. This stimulates mitochondrial biogenesis, improves insulin sensitivity, and enhances GLP-1 secretion. Think: incline walking, rucking, cycling, or nasal-breathing jogs.

🏋️‍♂️ Loaded Carries

  • Farmer’s carries, suitcase carries, overhead walks
  • Stimulate core, grip, and postural muscles
  • Trigger hormonal adaptations that support satiety and lean mass retention

🐒 Primal Patterns

Incorporate crawling, hanging, squatting, and ground transitions. These movements restore joint integrity, stimulate the vestibular system, and reconnect you with ancestral movement patterns.

⏱️ Minimum Effective Dose

  • 2–3 Zone 2 sessions per week (30–45 min)
  • 2–3 strength sessions with carries and bodyweight work
  • Daily movement snacks: walking, squatting, crawling
