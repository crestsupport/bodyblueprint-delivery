Nutrition: Leverage, Not Willpower

GLP-1 isn’t just triggered by what you eat — it’s shaped by how you build your plate. This tier introduces the science of protein leverage, fiber fermentation, and nutrient timing to help you activate satiety without restriction.

🥩 Protein Leverage Theory

Your body has a fixed protein target. If you under-consume protein, you’ll keep eating — even if you’ve had enough calories. Hitting your protein threshold early in the day is one of the most powerful ways to activate GLP-1 and reduce cravings.

  • Prioritize 30–50g of protein per meal
  • Use animal-based sources for complete amino acid profiles
  • Front-load protein at breakfast to blunt hunger all day

🌱 Fiber That Feeds GLP-1

Not all fiber is created equal. The right kinds — like those in zucchini, fennel, and cabbage — ferment in the gut and produce short-chain fatty acids (SCFAs) that stimulate GLP-1 release.

  • Choose low-inflammatory, fermentable fibers
  • Avoid kale and raw crucifers that irritate the gut lining
  • Combine fiber with healthy fats to slow digestion and enhance satiety

🧠 The GLP-1 Food Matrix

Every meal is a chance to send a hormonal signal. The Intermediary Tier emphasizes:

  • Protein-first sequencing (eat protein before carbs or fat)
  • Low-glycemic load (no blood sugar rollercoasters)
  • Anti-inflammatory fats (olive oil, ghee, tallow — never seed oils)
