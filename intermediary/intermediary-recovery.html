Recovery: Where Hormones Reset

GLP-1 isn’t just about what you eat — it’s about how you recover. Sleep, stress, and temperature all influence your body’s ability to regulate hunger and satiety. This module teaches you how to recover like your biology depends on it — because it does.

😴 Sleep Depth > Sleep Hours

  • Prioritize deep sleep (Stage 3) and REM — that’s when GLP-1 sensitivity resets
  • Block blue light 2 hours before bed
  • Keep your room cold, dark, and silent

❄️ Cold Exposure

Cold plunges, cold showers, or even brisk outdoor walks stimulate brown fat and increase GLP-1 secretion. Start with 30–60 seconds post-shower and build up gradually.

📉 Cortisol Modulation

  • High cortisol blunts GLP-1 and increases cravings
  • Use nasal breathing, sunlight exposure, and walking to lower baseline stress
  • Protect your mornings: no phone, no chaos, no sugar

🧬 Recovery Is a Hormonal Lever

When you recover deeply, your body stops hoarding. GLP-1 flows. Cravings fade. You become metabolically flexible — not just disciplined.
