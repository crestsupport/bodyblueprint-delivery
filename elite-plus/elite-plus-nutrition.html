Nutrition: Stack the Signal

At this tier, food isn’t fuel — it’s a hormonal message. You’ll learn how to cycle protein, modulate your microbiome, and stack GLP-1 activators for maximum metabolic control.

🔁 Protein Cycling

Constant high-protein intake can blunt GLP-1 sensitivity. Strategic cycling restores responsiveness and supports longevity.

  • Use high-protein days (2–3x/week) to trigger satiety and muscle repair
  • Use low-protein days (1–2x/week) to stimulate autophagy and receptor reset
  • Break low-protein fasts with collagen, bone broth, or fermented aminos

🦠 Microbiome Modulation

Your gut flora shape your GLP-1 response. At Elite-Plus, you feed the strains that amplify it.

  • Rotate fermentable fibers (e.g., fennel, cabbage, zucchini) to avoid monoculture
  • Use polyphenol-rich plants (e.g., rosemary, olives, blueberries) to support SCFA production
  • Include fermented foods 3–5x/week for microbial diversity

📈 GLP-1 Stacking

GLP-1 activation is exponential when signals are stacked. This tier teaches you how to layer them.

  • Sequence meals: protein → fiber → fat → carbs (if any)
  • Stack cold exposure + fasted movement + protein-first meals
  • Use bitter compounds (e.g., arugula, dandelion) to amplify gut signaling
