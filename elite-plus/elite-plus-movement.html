Movement: Stress the System, Then Recover

At this tier, movement isn’t just about burning calories — it’s about sending hormonal signals. You’ll learn how to use eccentric loading, sprint intervals, and cold-adapted locomotion to amplify GLP-1 and build metabolic resilience.

📉 Eccentric Loading

Muscle lengthening under load triggers myokines that stimulate GLP-1 and improve insulin sensitivity.

  • Use slow negatives in lifts (e.g., 4-second descent on squats or pull-ups)
  • Prioritize compound movements that challenge multiple joints
  • Recover with protein + electrolytes within 30 minutes

⚡ Sprint Intervals

Short bursts of max effort trigger GLP-1 release and improve mitochondrial efficiency.

  • Use 20–30 second sprints with 90–120 second recovery
  • Limit to 1–2 sessions per week to avoid cortisol overload
  • Stack with cold exposure for amplified hormonal response

❄️ Cold-Adapted Locomotion

Walking in cold environments (shirtless, minimal layers) activates brown fat and increases GLP-1 secretion.

  • Start with 10–15 minutes in 50–60°F (10–15°C) conditions
  • Use nasal breathing to stay parasympathetic
  • Combine with fasted state for maximum hormonal leverage
