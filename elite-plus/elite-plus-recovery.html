Recovery: Hormetic, Not Passive

At this tier, recovery isn’t rest — it’s stimulus. You’ll learn how to anchor your circadian rhythm, optimize glymphatic flow, and layer stressors to build resilience and amplify GLP-1.

🌅 Circadian Anchoring

Your biological clock controls GLP-1 sensitivity. When your rhythm is stable, your satiety signals are strong.

  • Get sunlight within 30 minutes of waking
  • Eat meals at consistent times — especially breakfast
  • Dim lights and block screens 2 hours before bed

🧠 Glymphatic Optimization

Deep sleep clears metabolic waste and resets GLP-1 pathways. But only if your glymphatic system is primed.

  • Sleep on your side to enhance cerebrospinal fluid flow
  • Use magnesium and glycine to deepen sleep architecture
  • Avoid alcohol and late meals — they block glymphatic clearance

🔥 Hormetic Stress Layering

Recovery isn’t the absence of stress — it’s the strategic layering of it. This builds resilience and amplifies GLP-1.

  • Use cold exposure + breathwork + fasted movement in sequence
  • Limit each stressor to 2–10 minutes — intensity matters more than duration
  • Recover with protein, electrolytes, and parasympathetic rituals (e.g., nasal breathing, nature)
