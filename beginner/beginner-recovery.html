Recovery Strategies: Beginner Tier

Recovery amplifies GLP-1 signaling and supports metabolic reset. This module introduces foundational strategies to improve sleep, reduce stress, and align your biology.

  • 🛏️ Sleep hygiene: consistent bedtime, blackout curtains, no screens 1 hour before sleep
  • 🌞 Circadian rhythm: morning sunlight exposure and evening wind-down routine
  • 🧘‍♂️ Breathwork: 4-7-8 breathing before meals and bedtime
These recovery strategies reduce cortisol, improve insulin sensitivity, and reinforce GLP-1 activation naturally.
