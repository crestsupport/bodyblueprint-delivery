Mindset & Habits: Beginner Tier

Transformation starts with identity. This module helps you build habits that reinforce your new GLP-1-powered lifestyle.

  • 🧠 Identity shift: “I am someone who nourishes my body and moves daily”
  • 📓 Daily reflection: What did I eat, how did I move, how did I sleep?
  • 📅 Habit stacking: Pair new habits with existing ones (e.g., walk after brushing teeth)
  • 🔁 Consistency over perfection: Progress compounds through repetition
Your mindset shapes your biology. These habits reinforce satiety, energy, and metabolic resilience.
