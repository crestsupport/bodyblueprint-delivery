Breakfast Recipes

Egg & Cauliflower Scramble

  • 3 pasture-raised eggs
  • 1 cup riced cauliflower
  • 1 tbsp ghee
  • Sea salt, turmeric

Prep: Scramble eggs in ghee. Add cauliflower and spices. Cook until fluffy.

GLP-1 Benefit: Protein + fiber = satiety and blood sugar stability.

Zucchini & Turkey Sausage Hash

  • Ground turkey
  • Diced zucchini
  • Olive oil
  • Garlic powder

Prep: Sauté turkey and zucchini in olive oil until browned.

GLP-1 Benefit: High protein + low-inflammatory veggie combo.

Sardine & Cucumber Stack

  • Wild sardines

  • Olive oil
