Nutrition Foundations: Beginner Tier

Nutrition is the cornerstone of GLP-1 activation. This tier focuses on simple, repeatable meals that reduce inflammation and amplify satiety.

  • 🍳 Prioritize protein at every meal — eggs, beef, chicken, fish
  • 🥦 Use low-inflammatory vegetables — zucchini, cucumber, spinach, cauliflower (no kale)
  • 🧂 Season with ancestral fats — olive oil, tallow, ghee
  • 🕒 Eat within a consistent window to support circadian rhythm
These meals support gut hormone signaling, reduce cravings, and stabilize blood sugar naturally.
