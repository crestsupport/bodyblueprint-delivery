Movement Protocols: Beginner Tier

Daily movement is essential for GLP-1 activation. This tier focuses on simple, accessible routines that require no gym and no equipment.

  • 🚶‍♂️ 20-minute walk after meals
  • 🎒 Ruck-lite: backpack with 10–15 lbs for 30 minutes
  • 💪 Bodyweight circuit: 3 rounds of squats, pushups, planks
These protocols stimulate gut hormone release, improve insulin sensitivity, and reinforce satiety signaling.
