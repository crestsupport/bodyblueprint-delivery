GLP-1 Activation: Beginner Tier

GLP-1 is your body’s natural satiety amplifier. This module introduces simple, daily habits to stimulate GLP-1 release without drugs or supplements.

  • 🥬 Eat protein-first meals with low-inflammatory vegetables (no kale)
  • 🚶‍♂️ Move after meals: 10–20 minute walk or light ruck
  • 🧘‍♀️ Breathe deeply before eating to activate parasympathetic response
These habits help regulate appetite, improve insulin sensitivity, and support long-term metabolic health.
